**Purpose:** This file is the working specification and source policy for *Gruppa Ost: Pobeda Za Nami*, a Hearts of Iron IV alternate-history mod. It combines the user's stated intent with evidence from the existing scripts and localization, marking whether each point is user-specified, code-confirmed, inferred, or not recorded.

**Status:** Working design brief — not a complete implementation specification. The authoritative project target is HOI4 `1.18.*`, matching `descriptor.mod` and the user's recollection. The 2026-10-07 startup diagnosis confirmed installed and logged build `1.18.3.0.7709`; reconfirm the runtime before future point-release-specific work.

## Approved source policy

### Allowed sources

Use only these sources to establish HOI4 scripting syntax, file placement, loading behavior, and game behavior:

1. **Hearts of Iron IV Wiki modding documentation:** <https://hoi4.paradoxwikis.com/Modding> and pages linked from it that specifically document modding. Verify that a page exists and is relevant before relying on it. Record the page title and the relevant section, not just the wiki home page.
2. **Paradox Interactive sources:** official HOI4 patch notes, developer diaries, and official support or game documentation, when they directly address the behavior in question.
3. **Version-matched vanilla game files:** files from the user's own installed copy of the target HOI4 version and enabled DLC. These are primary examples of how that version's content is authored. Record the relative in-game path and relevant entry or identifier; do not redistribute large vanilla file contents into the mod or this document.
4. **The user's existing mod files and test logs:** use these to establish this project's conventions and observed failures, not as universal documentation of HOI4 behavior.

The user may also provide selected files from other HOI4 mods (for example, TNO or Black ICE) as **design references** for interface organization, economic modeling, or production chains. Treat these as comparative examples of the user's desired feel or system shape only. They are not approved authority for vanilla HOI4 syntax or 1.18.* behavior. Keep reference material read-only and separate from the active mod; do not transplant their code, localization, or assets unless the user explicitly requests it and the material's license permits it. Prefer a small curated set of files with a manifest explaining the relevant mechanic over an entire mod dump. For every example, record its source mod/version, exact file or screen, what design property to study, and what Gruppa Ost should adapt in original form.

Do not use search snippets as evidence. Do not treat Reddit, Discord, YouTube, Fandom, generic tutorials, third-party mod repositories, or another model's answer as approved technical sources. If the user later approves an additional source, add it here with its scope and limits before relying on it.

The premise is fictional and user-authored. Separate scenario assumptions from claims about real people, institutions, conflicts, weapons, or historical events. The attached screenshots may contain user-written draft lore; do not cite or repeat it as verified history. Before adding real-world factual detail, ask the user to approve a factual-source list or use primary sources from the relevant governments and institutions, clearly separating sourced fact from speculation. #### Accounting units, GDP and update order

- Common accounting currency: reference USD. Store annual **real GDP in billions at constant 2027 prices**. The existing reserved `GO_econ_state_gdp` field will mean this real GDP; the separate nominal/display caches must never overwrite it. Treasury, payments and debt use billions of nominal reference USD.
- Each country has a positive national price index, starting at **1.00**, and an annual inflation input. Nominal GDP is real GDP multiplied by that index. Stage 3 advances the index once per successful month from an explicitly populated input: `index × (1 + annual_inflation_percent / 1200)`. This deliberate approximation does not generate inflation. Never subtract inflation directly from GDP or repeatedly deflate an already-real stock.
- Starting state real GDP is authored scenario data; **population already exists in the game per state**. Stage 3 refreshes native `state_population_k / 1000` into the guarded **millions** cache. The user does not need to supply another population dataset. There is no factory-count GDP proxy or invented world dataset. Available/recruitable military manpower is not this denominator. Real GDP per capita is `real_GDP_billions × 1000 / population_millions`. A nonpositive native population makes dependent results unavailable. National per-capita GDP divides the sum of eligible GDP by the population of the same states, rather than averaging state ratios.
- The starter annual real growth rate is **2.00%**, plus **0.25 percentage points per funded Government Services level**, plus explicit authored growth modifiers. Bound the combined annual rate to **−20.00% through +15.00%** for the pilot. Monthly state growth is `real_GDP × (1 + annual_real_growth_percent / 1200)`. Services affect growth, not an immediate GDP payment. Processing output and physical factories do not automatically add GDP in this contract; any later value-added model must avoid counting intermediate inputs twice.

#### Production units and native industry

**Starter capacity rule:** `potential_PUs = floor(max(0, national_base_PUs + completed_state_industry_bonus + operating_real_GDP_billions / 100))`. The national base starts at **10 PUs** and can be changed by authored effects. Each completed eligible state industry level adds **1 base PU**. `usable_PUs = min(potential_PUs, Global_Status_PU_cap)`. All components, the cap and the suppressed remainder are shown separately. Reaching the cap suppresses usable capacity; it does not destroy GDP, erase investments or promote the country.

The energy backend will expose validated operational availability for state contributions. Shortages may suspend affected state-derived capacity; they must not permanently delete GDP or completed industry levels. This contract supplies no generation, dispatch or transmission formula. The initial PU bridge prototype must label energy integration as absent and demonstrate its own arithmetic independently; it cannot claim that a neutral test availability value implements the energy system.

- **1 civilian PU → 1 offsite `industrial_complex`; 1 military PU → 1 offsite `arms_factory`.** No TNO-specific half-military conversion, random PU removal, trade-PU formula or custom consumer-goods formula is inherited. Keep native consumer-goods reservations for the first implementation and display total civil allocation versus factories actually available after native reservations.
- Initial requested allocation is **50% civilian / 50% military**. Clamp requests to whole percentages from **0 through 100**; players may request a different percentage, settled only at the next monthly update. Civil allocation is `floor(usable_PUs × requested_civil_percent / 100)`; the military allocation receives the remainder. Desired shares and actual factories are displayed separately.
- Capacity losses/status reductions apply immediately, preserving the last settled allocation ratio with the same deterministic rounding rule. Newly available capacity uses that settled ratio until the next allocation settlement. Emergency allocation changes require an authored event; repeated UI clicks cannot bypass the monthly cadence.
- The bridge records the civ/mil grants it owns, computes the required deltas and applies them once in country scope. Refreshing or reloading must not duplicate grants. Removal is bounded by its recorded grants and never consumes unrelated event-granted/physical factories. A dedicated starting-scenario migration must reconcile existing authored map civs/mils before the model replaces them; this documentation update performs no such migration.
- Civs/mils are not directly buildable and are not placed in individual state building slots by the new system. State industry levels are economic development records, not disguised map civs/mils. Existing production lines, factory resources and native construction capacity must be checked with the offsite grants in the target game. Dockyards remain physical construction outside this PU conversion.
- Never feed native civ/mil counts or PU grants into the new GDP calculation. The old weekly factory/resource helpers remain a separate prototype until an explicit migration retires them; joining the two models would create a GDP → PU → factory → GDP feedback loop.

#### Treasury, debt and the national report

For every authorized payment of cost `C`: `cash_used = min(treasury, C)`; `new_borrowing = C − cash_used`; `treasury -= cash_used`. Treasury remains nonnegative, and the shortfall becomes debt automatically. At zero treasury, the whole payment is borrowed; there is no separate player-confirmation step or invented borrowing cap in this contract. Borrowing is financing, not income.

Attribute state project/service borrowing to that state **and its legal debtor country**. The existing state debt field summarizes geographic liabilities; national debt is derived from the entries legally owed by the country, including liabilities located in territory it later loses. Country and state reports must not be two independently incremented copies of the same debt. Annexation/occupation must not silently forgive the old borrower's debt or charge it to the new owner. A later shared payment helper must accept a state attribution for state costs; non-state costs require an explicit attribution record rather than guessing a state's share.

The monthly report separates receipts, recurring Government Services, one-time development/construction capital spending, other populated budget categories, deficit/surplus and financing. `deficit = expenses − receipts`. Borrowing pays recorded expenses; reporting a deficit never borrows again. Refunds reverse the unspent portion of the original capital expense and are shown separately; they are not taxable operating income. Income increases treasury, and does not automatically repay existing loans in this contract. The contract adds shortfall financing and project-specific refund retirement only; interest, general repayment and default systems are not added by implication.

Government Services appear in the **macro economy screen**, with funded/completed levels, growth benefit, monthly cost and the effect of suspending funding. Their cost flows into national expenses and deficit once. The same screen shows GDP, cash/debt, PU components, Global Status, allocation and state reporting coverage. A visible Resources/Trade control opens the related resource screen, with a return control. State details retain the same underlying records; switching screens never settles a month or charges a project.

#### Update and accounting path

New-game startup calls `GO_econ_initialize_economy_fiscal`. Existing saves need an explicit authored call to that idempotent wrapper; loading alone does not reset schemas, month/day tokens, progress or stocks. Configuration defaults live in `GO_economy_fiscal_foundation.txt`, preserve authored overrides, and reject invalid divisors/growth bounds/fractional project durations.

The native `on_monthly` pulse advances `global.GO_econ_month_serial` once per native world day and attempts only authorized unsettled countries. Preflight refreshes native population/debt and requires all owned-and-controlled states' GDP, modifier, completed/requested levels and complete liabilities, plus valid country fiscal/status inputs. **Pilot coverage reconciliation (D-014):** Earlier generic pilot language is not an exemption from that full-country preflight. A few-state pilot within a country that owns other controlled states is blocked by design, even if all selected states are populated. An isolated pilot can instead supply synthetic inputs for every eligible state of its isolated country. Authorizing a narrower eligibility set would be a separate design decision, not a Stage 4 bug fix. The host evaluator uses complete synthetic fixture-country coverage and never changes campaign eligibility.

For a successful month, set the country last-month and in-progress guards before committing, then follow the approved order:

1. Apply pending country suspension and each eligible state's requested funded levels together. Existing completed assets are retained.
2. Add only explicit current-serial receipts to treasury; record receipts separately and consume their readiness markers.
3. Charge services from **opening** real GDP/index: `GDP × 0.005 × funded_levels / 12 × price_index`. Spend treasury first and attribute the shortfall to the state's key for its legal owner. Record expense, cash use and new financing separately. Each state has a last-service-month guard.
4. Refresh the bounded growth projection and copy it to real GDP once, guarded by that state's last-growth-month token. This also prevents a same-month ownership transfer from growing/billing the state twice. Report refresh never performs this step.
5. Advance the price index once using the explicitly populated annual inflation input. No inflation-generation model is introduced.
6. Close pending event flows once and refresh state GDP, country GDP/debt and the historical fiscal report. The PU allocation/bridge portion of the wider contract remains deferred.

Event payments/refunds immediately change cash and canonical debt and accumulate distinct country `*_pending` entries: capital expenses, capital refunds, cash used, borrowing, refunds, debt retired and refund cash. A successful settlement moves each to `*_last` and clears pending once. `open_flow_first_day`/`open_flow_last_day` persist the actual pending event span; closed `report_flow_first_day`/`report_flow_last_day`/`report_closed_day` preserve it even across blocked months. `receipts_last` and `services_expenses_last` remain separate. `expenses = services + capital − capital_refunds`; `deficit = expenses − receipts` (negative is surplus); `financing = borrowing`, never income or another loan caused by the displayed deficit. Refund totals are displayed separately and do not reverse expenses a second time.

`GO_econ_refresh_fiscal_report` only rebuilds the **last closed pilot ledger**. It cannot settle or consume open flows. If a settlement was skipped, retained capital/refund flows may span earlier days; they close once at the next successful settlement, rather than being erased or claimed as a complete calendar-month/world budget. This is a Stage 3 account, excluding unimplemented tax/trade/other budget categories. Its guard is not permission to claim a complete economy report or display unavailable GDP, treasury or debt. No general debt repayment, interest, debt cap or default mechanic is added.

Stage 4 requires a complete nonnegative pending ledger and valid recorded flow span before payments, refunds or settlement. A complete closed ledger, including its flow/close dates, is required before publishing a historical fiscal report. Missing historical inputs invalidate report readiness while preserving remaining stored values. A later successful settlement can replace a historical record with a new complete close; initialization cannot invent its missing receipts or financing.

The centrally editable **`global.GO_econ_cfg_gdp_income_factor = 0.20`**, initialized only when absent by `GO_econ_initialize_fiscal_config`, yields:

`monthly GDP income = complete opening real GDP × opening price index × annual income factor / months per year`

The existing months-per-year setting remains **12**. This is fictional gameplay calibration, not a tax-law system or a direct GDP-stock cash credit. Authored countries without the new income flag keep their original explicit monthly-receipt behavior. Partial, stale or populated conflicting original inputs block preflight rather than disappearing. The four starter profiles explicitly use policy 1. No extra tax/trade receipts are assumed.

The original monthly path rebuilds complete opening GDP, validates the GDP-income amount and prospective treasury before any financial/GDP write, claims its existing serial, then credits the combined income **once** in the original income stage. Services payments, automatic shortfall borrowing, growth, inflation/index update, capital spending/refunds and close order are preserved. **Expenditure = existing Services + recorded capital spending − capital refunds; deficit = expenditure − income.** A negative deficit is a surplus.
